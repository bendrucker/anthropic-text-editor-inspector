# Fringing Reef Survey, Third Season

Transects were repeated at the same six stations using the original 2023 markers. Coverage is reported as a percentage of the transect line, so the numbers compare directly to the two prior seasons.

## Coral Cover by Station


| Station | Hard Coral | Soft Coral | Algae | Bleached |
| ------- | ---------- | ---------- | ----- | -------- |
| North 1 | 41%        | 12%        | 22%   | 8%       |
| North 2 | 38%        | 9%         | 26%   | 11%      |
| Mid 1   | 52%        | 14%        | 14%   | 6%       |
| Mid 2   | 47%        | 11%        | 18%   | 9%       |
| South 1 | 29%        | 7%         | 38%   | 18%      |
| South 2 | 24%        | 6%         | 42%   | 18%      |


The two South stations continue to diverge from the rest of the reef. Algae cover there is roughly double the northern stations, and Bleached cover at 18% is the highest recorded at any station in three seasons.

## Change Since 2023


| Station | Hard Coral | Bleached | Net       |
| ------- | ---------- | -------- | --------- |
| North 1 | -3%        | +2%      | Stable    |
| North 2 | -6%        | +5%      | Declining |
| Mid 1   | +1%        | 0%       | Stable    |
| Mid 2   | -2%        | +3%      | Stable    |
| South 1 | -14%       | +11%     | Declining |
| South 2 | -17%       | +12%     | Declining |


Three of six stations are Declining. The pattern is not uniform across the reef, and the southern decline is steep enough that it drives the whole-reef average on its own.

## Temperature Loggers


| Station | Mean  | Max   | Days Above 30C |
| ------- | ----- | ----- | -------------- |
| North 1 | 27.4C | 30.8C | 4              |
| Mid 1   | 27.6C | 31.1C | 7              |
| South 1 | 28.9C | 32.4C | 26             |


South 1 spent 26 days above the 30C threshold against 4 days at North 1. That gap tracks the Bleached cover closely enough that temperature is the obvious first explanation, though the survey design cannot separate it from the freshwater outflow at the southern end.

## Recommendation

Keep all six stations on the annual schedule and add a seventh between Mid 2 and South 1, where the transition appears to happen. Without a station in that gap the survey cannot say whether the boundary is sharp or gradual.
