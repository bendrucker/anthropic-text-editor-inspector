# North Ridge Trail Conditions

Field notes from the September survey. Each segment was walked end to end and rated against the same four-point scale, so the ratings compare directly across the network.

## Rating Scale

- **Clear** — no obstruction, tread intact for the full width
- **Passable** — usable with care, minor blowdown or washout
- **Restricted** — passable only with detour or scramble
- **Closed** — unsafe, signed at both ends

## Segment Notes

- **Lower Ridge**, mile 0.0 to 2.4
  - Tread is Clear for the first mile, then Passable where the spring crossing has cut a new channel
  - Two blowdowns near the switchbacks, both stepped over rather than cut
  - Blazes repainted in August and still legible
- **Saddle Traverse**, mile 2.4 to 5.1
  - Passable throughout, with loose scree on the north-facing pitch
  - The cairn at the saddle has been rebuilt twice this season and is again scattered
  - Water is reliable at the spring 200 feet below the saddle
- **Upper Basin**, mile 5.1 to 7.8
  - Restricted above the talus field, where the old cut has slid out entirely
  - A boot path has formed 30 feet uphill and is now the de facto route
  - Snow lingered until late July, which is three weeks past the ten-year median
- **Summit Spur**, mile 7.8 to 8.3
  - Passable, though the final scramble is exposed and unmarked
  - Register box is intact, pencil missing again

## Work Priorities

The Upper Basin slide is the only segment where the published route no longer exists on the ground. Rerouting it onto the boot path would take a crew of six about four days, most of that spent on retaining cribbing below the talus.

Everything else on the network is Passable or better and needs routine clearing rather than construction. The Saddle Traverse cairn should be rebuilt with larger stone, since two rebuilds in one season suggests the current stone is simply too small to hold.
